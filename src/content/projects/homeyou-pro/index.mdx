### About the Project

Homeyou PRO is the professional-facing arm of Homeyou — a platform connecting homeowners with home service professionals across the United States. The PRO landing page serves as the primary acquisition channel for service providers, communicating the platform's value proposition with clarity and confidence.

The goal was to deliver a premium, conversion-focused experience that feels as polished as the brand it represents — combining fluid animations with a clean, fast interface.

### Assignment

Design and develop the end-to-end landing page experience for Homeyou PRO, working closely with the design and product teams to ensure pixel-perfect implementation, performance, and responsiveness across all devices.

### Stack

Built with **Next.js** and **TypeScript** for a robust, type-safe foundation. **Motion One** powers the smooth entrance animations and scroll-driven interactions, while **Tailwind CSS** ensures a consistent, maintainable design system throughout.

### Outcome

Delivered a fast, animated, and responsive landing page that clearly communicates the Homeyou PRO value proposition — supporting professional acquisition and strengthening the brand's credibility with service providers.
