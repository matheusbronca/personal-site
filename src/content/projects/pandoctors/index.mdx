### Intro

Building high-performance websites demands a seamless fusion of design and development. As a Fullstack Engineer, I led the creation of the Pan Drs. platform, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for users seeking healthcare professionals.

### About the Project

Pan Drs. is a web app designed to simplify the process of finding and connecting with doctors. The platform prioritizes accessibility, ease of navigation, and a clean, intuitive interface to enhance user engagement.

### Assignment

I was responsible for the complete development cycle—from visual design and UX strategy to technical implementation. Every aspect of the website and webapp was thoughtfully crafted to deliver a polished experience that balances aesthetics with functionality.

### Outcome

The result is a high-performance website that excels in both usability and technical benchmarks, earning top scores in PageSpeed and Lighthouse metrics. This project highlights my ability to merge thoughtful design with robust development, delivering efficient and visually refined digital solutions.
